The khans

Fourteen named seats. One role each. The assembly still requires a quorum.

A public roll of the Kurultai agent fleet, kept by Jebe.

01 · The compact

A kurultai chose the khans. The names on this page are how you find a seat, not a claim on the thirteenth century. Each bot keeps a generic role. The sidebar name is the one you open.

Agents propose. Gates decide. Nothing here sends, spends, or ships without a yes.
